# ELO Ranking Suggestions

Suggested Improvements: 
•	Set a minimum rating limit (like a floor ranking) after which a player’s rating cannot drop further in Tic-Tac-Toe to prevent excessive demotions due to frequent draws.
•	Once a player reaches a certain rating, the K-factor should remain constant to ensure fair ranking adjustments and prevent new players from gaining an advantage over experienced players.
•	Players should not continuously lose points for unavoidable draws at high levels, maintaining a more stable and competitive ranking system.
•	These adjustments would help create a fairer ranking progression for Tic-Tac-Toe.

# Use Case Descriptions & Diagram

**Seasonal Leaderboards:**
Seasonal leaderboards keep things fresh and exciting by giving players new challenges to compete for. With each season, there’s a new 
opportunity to climb the ranks, which keeps players engaged and coming back. It’s a great way to boost competition and keep the 
community active. This would boost player base engagement and increase their engagement.

**Matchmaking History:**
Being able to see your last 20 matches (or more) would make it easier to track progress and spot trends in your gameplay. A matchmaking
history gives players a better sense of how they’re doing over time.
